## Metric definitions

Recall@5 uses authorized relevance (semantic relevance intersected with the caller's allowed tools). The primary macro denominator includes only queries with 1..5 authorized relevant tools. Queries with >5 tools are reported separately with their theoretical Recall ceiling, min(5 / relevant count, 1). Empty sets never enter Recall/Hit/MRR.

Hit@5 is the fraction with at least one relevant top-five result. MRR@5 is truncated: 1 / first relevant rank for ranks 1..5, otherwise 0. Errors contribute zero quality and retain their measured latency. False recommendation counts any nonempty response to an empty-relevance query; correct empty response additionally requires no request error. These retrieval statistics do not measure an Agent's refusal quality.

All intervals are seeded query-bootstrap percentile 95% CIs. Missing denominators are N/A; one-query and constant samples yield degenerate intervals. Group proportions refer to all queries; cohort proportions refer to the containing group. Small cohorts remain visible. Unauthorized counts cover every returned position, including positions after 5.
